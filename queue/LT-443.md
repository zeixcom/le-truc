**Changed:** A scalar signal seed declares its harvest parser with a second `harvest()` overload — `createState(harvest(price, asNumber()))` (ADR 0046 s7) — beside LT-429's list map in `@zeix/le-truc-compiler/macros` (`harvest<T>(seed: T, parser: FieldParser<T>): T`). Every scalar harvest read takes it: the direct text/attribute site, the substituted DOM read of a seed that IS the arg (a `Date` round-trips this way through `asParser`), and a membership value read. The parser splices into the client as authored; its free names must resolve on the client (LTC005's client-position face). New **LTC077** (compiler, tier 1 Prevented, no runtime half): a scalar-seeded signal with a raw harvest read whose seed type the compiler cannot read (anything but the bare `string`/`number`/`boolean` keyword) and no marker. A marker no read takes (literal seed, verbatim-reused initializer, deriving seed) is refused, as are mismatched marker/constructor pairings and malformed arity. A number-valued marker (`asNumber`/`asInteger`/`asClampedInteger`, matched by name) coerces the signal's reactive attribute/property sites through `String()`, as an inferred `number` does. Corpus census: zero components refused. Docs: HOST_PROFILE (*Scalar harvest*, markers), LE_TRUC_COMPILER, VOCABULARY_LEDGER, CHANGELOG, `skills/le-truc` errors.md + compiled.md.
**Review:** Approved after one rework round (Architect, 2026-10-06). Rulings: (1) on the substitute limb the parser and LTC077 apply only to an identity seed — a deriving seed (`value.length`) re-evaluates identically on both sides and is never stringy; the fix-it would miscompile it (recorded in HOST_PROFILE *Scalar harvest*); (2) the marker inside an `expose()` entry stays LTC005 — a plain-value prop's client seed is evaluated once and cannot parse a DOM read; `expose({ value: harvest(…) })` would be a new feature, not a spelling. Rework finding 1 (the wrapped seed's attribute site failed tsc) fixed in 0f548505, pinned on both surfaces and in `client.golden.test.ts`'s emit-then-check. LTC077 and the marker refusal copy are a first draft — `writer` pass at iteration close.
