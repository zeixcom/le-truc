**Area:** compiler
**Changed:** ADR 0048 s4, the content model. **LTC085** (tier 1 Prevented, compiler, both surfaces): a compose site of a child whose `children` declares `Children<…, 'non-interactive'>` fails when its literal children contain interactive content — `a[href]`, `button`, `input` (except `type="hidden"`, case-insensitive), `select`, `textarea`, `label`, `details`, `iframe`, any `[tabindex]`, `audio`/`video` with `controls`; a dynamically bound deciding attribute counts as present — or compose a child whose template renders any, transitively. The message names the offending element or component and the child's declaration. Registry entries gain `interactive: boolean` (own template, including markup the component passes at its own compose sites, transitively through composed children; content passed TO it never counts) and `childrenModel` (present exactly when the annotation is). The model argument reads through a same-file alias; an unreadable one keeps the check off. Page-authored HTML is unchecked and TypeScript cannot carry the check. Code: `server/compiler/analysis/content-model.ts`. Docs: HOST_PROFILE, LE_TRUC_COMPILER, VOCABULARY_LEDGER, `skills/le-truc/references/errors.md`.
**Review:** Approved (2026-10-09) after one rework: (1) the `interactive` flag now counts markup a component passes at its own compose sites; (2) the hidden-input exemption folds case. Accepted as is: the composed-component leg co-occurs with LTC011 today (forward-correct once nesting in compose content is admitted); "literal children" stops at a nested compose site; dynamic `controls` counts as present; an unknown model literal records `'any'` (tsc rejects it). LT-479 consumes the element leg.
